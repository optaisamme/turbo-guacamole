# Your settings

These two files hold your settings. **You do not need to edit them.** The setup questionnaire fills
them in: in your copy, open the **Issues** tab, click **New issue**, then **Job search setup**
([open the questionnaire list](../../../issues/new/choose)).

To change something, click **click here to change these settings** at the top of the **JOB_SEARCH_RESULTS**
folder page. It opens the questionnaire with your current text answers filled in.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `job_search_settings.toml` | Job titles, where you can work, salary, words and employers to skip, certifications, companies to watch, skills-based matching, how many jobs to look through, automatic weekly searches |
| `my_experience.toml` | Your skills, years of experience and certifications (optional). Used to show how well each job fits you; never removes a job |
